# Pilot 001: input discovery and linkage resolution

**Checked:** 2026-10-23. **Evidence class:** executed public-metadata and source
checks; no model fitting or reproduced performance result. This follows the
[static feasibility audit](pilot-001-feasibility-audit.md) and records what is
resolved at the catalog level versus what is needed for numerical reproduction.

**Main finding:** the pinned public supplemental score/clinical records recover
the 64-protein panel and the reported analysis denominators, but six patient keys
in the 34-row frozen `nonoverlap` score sheet also occur in the published
135-row discovery score set. This is a reproducible membership discrepancy
requiring clarification, not a measurement of its effect on performance.

totals were checked. `uiFilesPerStudy` then supplied the matching public file

line was read (4,573 / 524 / 1,691 bytes respectively). No full PDC abundance
matrix was downloaded and no whole-file checksum was independently verified.
Signed URLs and header identifiers were not retained.

These are **candidate upstream inputs**, not the missing author RData
workspaces or the alignment script's named batch-corrected/imputed TSVs. Exact
header matching against PDC case/sample/aliquot submitter IDs found zero matches
for each file. That is a mapping gap, not proof the files are unrelated. The
measurement-column encoding, repeated measurements, annotation schema and
transformation chain still need reconciliation; do not invent substring rules.

## Catalog case overlap

The [executed aggregate record](../registries/pilot-001-linkage-summary.json)
was generated by [audit_pdc_metadata.py](../tools/audit_pdc_metadata.py).
Only canonical case/sample/aliquot IDs, project context, reference and pool flags
were requested. Raw linkage rows remained in memory and were not written to the
checkout, logs, chat, or a patient manifest.

| Cohort | Catalog records | Reference records removed | Non-reference case records | Non-reference records flagged pooled |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Discovery | 161 | 3 | 158 | 16 |
| FFPE validation | 21 | 1 | 20 | 0 |
| Frozen validation | 66 | 2 | 64 | 0 |

Among non-reference records, discovery and frozen validation share **43 exact
canonical PDC case IDs** in consistent project context. The other **21 frozen
case records have no observed discovery match**. The FFPE validation records
share no case IDs with either cohort in this query.

These are catalog case-record counts **before prediction eligibility
exclusions**. They are not counts of verified distinct people or a locked
independent test cohort. The [PDC case model](https://pdc-docs.cancer.gov/pdc-docs/data-dictionary/case)
relates a subject to a project; different IDs alone do not prove different
people. A pool may combine cases, samples or aliquots. The 16 pooled discovery
records were retained and flagged; they must not be equated with 16 patients or
with the paper's mixed primary/metastatic exclusion category.

The observed 43 catalog matches are consistent with the paper's 29 overlapping
samples plus 14 additional same-patient tumor pairs. That arithmetic does not
establish their exact correspondence or the overlap remaining after exclusions.

To rerun the public metadata check without writing linkage rows:

```sh
python3 tools/audit_pdc_metadata.py
```

The tool checks exact version 1 study identities, complete required fields,
canonical flags, catalog counts, duplicate aliquots and conflicting case/sample/
aliquot mappings. It rereads study metadata after all linkage queries and stops
on version/count/identity drift. Output includes public study UUIDs, query hashes
and whole-response fingerprints; errors omit remote bodies and row details.
These checks detect specified inconsistencies, not all possible portal errors,
and do not create a transactional snapshot or establish biological independence.

## Recovered panel and supplemental cohort membership

The [official NLM cloud distribution](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/README.txt)
and [article-version metadata](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC10414761.1/PMC10414761.1.json)
provide versioned public supplemental files. The article metadata identify
CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, distinct from PDC's terms. Source workbooks were inspected only
in memory for this research task; neither whole workbooks nor patient rows are
redistributed. Source byte counts, MD5 and computed SHA-256 values are retained
in the [executed supplemental summary](../registries/pilot-001-supplement-summary.json).

| Input | Exact artifact and inspected location | Resolved contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clinical annotations | `NIHMS1918576-supplement-MMC1.xlsx`; `FFPE_Discovery_clinical`, `Frozen_clinical`, `FFPE_Pilot_clinical` | Explicit patient/sample keys, response, neoadjuvant status, location group, sample type and measurement-label join key. |
| Final protein panel | `NIHMS1918576-supplement-MMC4.xlsx`; `64_protein_predictors!A2:A65`, header `Proteins` | 64 unique nonblank symbols in source order. Canonical SHA-256: `77329920b86dacaffc6778ed7e7c6b827683beed1aad7f2229b8bafe2f535144` (UTF-8, LF after every symbol, no header). |
| Published prediction membership | The same MMC4 workbook; `FFPE_pred_scores`, `FFPE_valid_pred_scores`, `Frozen_nonoverlap_pred_scores`, `Frozen_overlap_pred_scores` | Respectively 135, 20, 34 and 27 rows, with headers `Samples` and `Pred.scores`. Scores were not used to calculate performance. |

The article XML's Table S3 cross-reference points to MMC3, but that workbook
contains pathway/clustering material. The panel was located by inspecting
workbook contents and its table-of-contents description, not by assuming that
an MMC filename equals a published table number. The recovered source specifies
the panel; it does not establish byte identity with the absent author CSV.

The clinical worksheets contain measurement rows and explicit `NA` records.
Only response-labelled biological rows were selected; rows whose `Tumor response`
was `NA` were required
to have explicit `NA` identity and relevant clinical fields. Repeated exact
`Sample ID` records were collapsed only after confirming agreement in `Patient ID`,
`Neo-adjuvant`, `Tumor response`, `Tumor Location Group` and `Sample type`.
There were zero conflicts. Each resulting cohort maps one-to-one from its
unique sample keys to its unique published patient keys.

Here **eligible** means membership reconstructed by excluding explicit
`Neo-adjuvant=yes` or `Tumor Location Group=Mix` records and checking exact
agreement with the published score sets. Discovery has two unique response-labelled
samples with location group `NA`; one remains after those exclusions. Literal
`NA` is retained as missing, not evidence of a nonmixed specimen. Frozen and FFPE
validation have no missing location groups among response-labelled samples.
Matching the published membership does not resolve this annotation gap or
establish complete clinical eligibility.

| Cohort | Response-labelled measurement rows | Unique sample/patient keys | Explicit exclusions | Eligible samples; sensitive/refractory |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Discovery | 168 | 158 | 13 `Neo-adjuvant=yes`; 10 `Tumor Location Group=Mix`; intersection 0 | 135; 83/52 |
| Frozen | 72 | 64 | 3 `Neo-adjuvant=yes`; no `Mix` | 61; 42/19 |
| FFPE validation | 20 | 20 | No neoadjuvant or `Mix` exclusions | 20; 10/10 |

Each score-sheet `Samples` key was joined by **exact equality** to the clinical
column `Data Label 2 <data set>_<TMTplex>_<sample source>_<patient ID>_sample ID>`.
Patient linkage then used the explicit `Patient ID` value, without splitting,
substring matching or normalization. All 135/20/34/27 score keys joined exactly
once: zero duplicates, missing matches or ambiguous matches. Discovery and FFPE
score membership exactly equal their reconstructed sample sets; the two
disjoint frozen score sets together exactly equal the 61 eligible frozen samples.

| Frozen population | Rows / unique published patient keys | Shared with eligible discovery 135 | Shared with full discovery 158 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Published `nonoverlap` score sheet | 34 | **6** | 13 |
| Published `overlap` score sheet | 27 | 27 | 27 |
| Both eligible frozen score sheets | 61 | 33 | 40 |

Thus **28** eligible frozen patient keys have no observed match to eligible
discovery, and **21** have no observed match to any discovery record. The latter
count also agrees with the pre-exclusion PDC comparison. Neither subset is yet a
verified independent evaluation: excluded discovery samples may still have
contributed to feature construction, preprocessing or the 43-pair assay alignment.
Do not silently relabel the published groups, infer a performance change, or
describe this as misconduct. Preserve the published groups in the reproduction
track and reconcile the membership discrepancy before a revised evaluation.

The reproducible, aggregate-only check is:

```sh
python3 tools/audit_published_cohorts.py
```

It retrieves two pinned public workbooks into memory, verifies byte counts and
hashes, validates selected clinical categories, checks replicate consistency and
exact joins, verifies the panel, and emits provenance/counts only. It does not
write patient keys, labels, scores, linkage maps or workbooks to disk. A successful
run establishes these source-file relationships, not the contents of the absent
author analysis workspaces or how the original models were fitted.

## Correction and retrieval limits

The available NLM figure supplement `NIHMS1918576-supplement-1.pdf` was pinned:
13,076,963 bytes, SHA-256
`7726253a4b85e7f1aae49b4bc68ed882004b56b3bd54678053d98dbdfec26c0c`.
It contains 13 pages and figures S1–S7. Its PDF modification metadata precedes
the 2024 correction and the inspected text does not explicitly declare corrected
legends. **Corrected status remains unverified**; timestamps alone cannot resolve
that question.

The Europe PMC `supplementaryFiles` endpoint returned HTTP 200 with ZIP headers,
but the actual response was only 162 bytes with a zero-byte GIF entry and no
workbooks. Successful HTTP metadata was not sufficient evidence of an available
supplement. PMC HTML routes returned shells/challenges and publisher routes were
inaccessible; the documented NLM public distribution supplied the inspected files.
The paper's Dropbox `processed_data.zip` link is labelled LC-MRM-MS material and
was not treated as the missing global-proteome input archive.

## Upstream reconstruction search

The author repository's current main remains
`48357fd63ccbfe3954f38c8efc558b4f88bd6051`. Read-only searches inspected all 42
R/r/Rmd files, 69 reachable upstream commits with 66 distinct complete trees,
and three public forks. No missing `balanced_folds.R`, authoritative

was recovered from those trees. Tags and releases were empty. The existing
[upstream issue asking for analysis data](https://github.com/WangLab-MSSM/CPTAC_Ovarian_Chemo_Response/issues/1)

analysis code was executed or copied.

## What remains before numerical reproduction

- Obtain the actual prediction-workspace inputs, their generation steps and
  exact object/column ordering, or document a reviewed reconstruction from
  sufficiently upstream data as a separate method.
- Recover `balanced_folds.R`, the original execution environment and the intended
  feature-selection/alignment implementations; reconcile the static discrepancies
  already recorded in the first audit.
- Resolve why six published `nonoverlap` patient keys occur in eligible discovery,
  and establish which samples contributed to every preprocessing/alignment step.
  Clarify the missing location annotation retained in discovery score membership.
- Confirm which available supplements incorporate the corrected legends.

The [unsent source-clarification request](../templates/pilot-001-source-request.md)
asks for these specific items without publishing patient identifiers. No authors
or collaborators have been contacted. Original code/workspace availability and
scientific interpretation still prevent claiming a faithful numerical reproduction.
