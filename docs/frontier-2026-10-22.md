# Ovarian cancer research frontier

Evidence snapshot: **2026-10-22**. This is a selective starting map, not a
systematic review. Source IDs and verification limits are in the
[source registry](../registries/sources.json). Research only; no patient care
recommendations follow from this document.

## Established clinical evidence

There are meaningful treatment advances in restricted populations. The FDA's
March 22, 2024 notice covers mirvetuximab soravtansine-gynx for adults with
FRα-positive, platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary
peritoneal cancer after one to three prior systemic regimens, selected using an
FDA-approved test. This is a biomarker-defined indication, not a cure claim.
[FDA notice, S04](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-mirvetuximab-soravtansine-gynx-fra-positive-platinum-resistant-epithelial-ovarian)

The FDA's February 10, 2026 notice covers pembrolizumab, and pembrolizumab with
berahyaluronidase alfa-pmph, combined with paclitaxel, with or without bevacizumab,
for adults with platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary
peritoneal carcinoma, PD-L1 CPS ≥1 by an FDA-authorized test, after one or two
prior systemic regimens. Neither approval should be attributed to AI on the
basis of these notices.
[FDA notice, S05](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-paclitaxel-platinum-resistant-epithelial-ovarian-fallopian-tube-or)

## Experimental evidence and useful questions

| Direction | What the evidence supports | Contribution and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial chemotherapy refractoriness | A 2023 proteogenomic study reports a 64-protein HGSOC signature. Its discovery cross-validation sensitivity was 0.35 (95% CI 0.19–0.52) at 98% specificity. | Reproduce a reported result and test robustness. Specificity is **not accuracy**; many refractory tumors are missed. This is not clinical validation. [S01](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.07.004) |
| Resistance and clonal evolution | Longitudinal single-cell and cell-free DNA work tracks selection of pre-existing clones during HGSOC treatment. | Study provenance, longitudinal sampling, and reproducibility. Mechanistic observations do not demonstrate that an adaptive treatment strategy improves survival. [S02](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09580-0) |
| Spatial biology and perturbation | Spatial profiling and functional perturbations connect malignant cell states with immune exclusion and immune-cell killing in experimental systems. | Ask which associations survive patient, site, and assay variation. Perturbation results are not demonstrated survival improvement in patients. [S03](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41590-024-01943-5) |
| Ultrasound AI | An international retrospective study assesses characterization of existing ovarian lesions. | Audit case selection, reference standards, and patient/site separation. This is not evidence for asymptomatic population screening or mortality benefit. [S06](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-03329-4) |

The Cell paper explicitly distinguishes overlapping discovery/frozen samples
from independent patients. The FFPE validation analysis also re-estimates its
model with cross-validation because alignment was not readily possible; it
should not be described as a locked-model external test. These distinctions
motivate an audit, not an allegation. The linked 2024 correction concerns
supplemental figure legends; use the corrected supplements when freezing a
reproduction target. Its text was verified through NCBI's BioC service after the
publisher/PMC routes were inaccessible.
[Paper manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10414761/),
[correction notice, S11](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2024.01.016)

## Hypotheses and proposed work

Our working hypothesis is that a transparent reproduction and a careful
patient-independent evaluation can clarify where this published signature is
robust and where its performance depends on cohort or assay choices. That is a
proposed methodological contribution, not an observed finding.

AI can help inspect code, curate evidence, and compare models. Its usefulness
must be measured against simple baselines with patient-independent evaluation.
Keep high-grade serous, low-grade serous, clear-cell, endometrioid, and mucinous
disease distinct unless pooling has a documented biological and statistical
justification. Do not transfer a signature between histotypes by assumption.

Early detection remains a separate problem. NCI summarizes randomized evidence
that studied CA-125/ultrasound screening strategies did not reduce ovarian cancer
mortality in average-risk populations. Better lesion classification alone does
not establish a screening benefit.
[NCI screening PDQ, S07](https://www.cancer.gov/types/ovarian/hp/ovarian-screening-pdq)
