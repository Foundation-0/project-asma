## Theorem P01 (Abundance-Free Disjoint Ion Matching and Helly-Farkas Exclusion)

**Statement (`VERIFIED`).** Let a candidate peptide $P$ generate $M$ theoretical fragment masses $\{\mu_1, \dots, \mu_M\} \subset \mathbb{Q}$, and let $S = \{m_1, \dots, m_K\} \subset \mathbb{Q}$ be an observed MS2 peak-mass list without peak intensities. Under shared instrument calibration offset $b \in [-\beta, +\beta] \cap \mathbb{Q}$ and per-peak random error $|e_p| \le \delta \in \mathbb{Q}_{\ge 0}$, candidate $P$ admits $m_{\min}$ physically disjoint explained fragment ions if and only if there exists at least one finite endpoint knot
$$b^* \in \{-\beta, +\beta\} \cup \bigcup_{i,p} \{\max(-\beta, m_p - \mu_i - \delta), \min(+\beta, m_p - \mu_i + \delta)\}$$
at which the unit-capacity bipartite flow network $G(P, S, b^*)$ (with peak-split capacity $c(p_{\text{in}}, p_{\text{out}}) = 1$) achieves maximum flow $F^*(P, b^*) \ge m_{\min}$. Conversely, if at every knot $b_r$ there exists an $s$-$t$ cut $(S_r, V \setminus S_r)$ of capacity $C(S_r) < m_{\min}$, then no calibration $b \in [-\beta, +\beta]$ (even over $\mathbb{R}$) can explain $m_{\min}$ disjoint peaks for $P$.

**Proof.**
1. *Unit-capacity peak non-collision:* Any valid physical assignment maps each predicted ion $i$ to at most one observed peak $p$ and assigns each observed peak $p$ to at most one ion under fixed charge state. Splitting each peak $p$ into $(p_{\text{in}} \to p_{\text{out}})$ of capacity $1$ establishes a bijection between size-$k$ matchings and integer $s$-$t$ flows of value $k$.
2. *Exact 1-D Helly reduction:* Edge $(i, p)$ is valid at offset $b \in [-\beta, +\beta]$ iff $b \in I_{i,p} = [\max(-\beta, m_p - \mu_i - \delta), \min(+\beta, m_p - \mu_i + \delta)]$. For any fixed matching $M'$ of size $k$, $M'$ is jointly valid at some $b \in \mathbb{R}$ iff $\bigcap_{(i,p) \in M'} I_{i,p} \neq \emptyset$. Because each $I_{i,p}$ is a closed bounded rational interval, any nonempty intersection $\bigcap_{(i,p) \in M'} I_{i,p} = [L_{M'}, U_{M'}]$ contains its rational left endpoint $L_{M'}$, which equals either $-\beta$ or $\max(-\beta, m_p - \mu_i - \delta)$ for some $(i, p) \in M'$. Hence the maximum flow over all real $b \in [-\beta, +\beta]$ is attained on the finite rational knot set.
3. *Soundness of cut exclusion without intensities:* At each knot $b_r$, by weak max-flow min-cut duality, any $s$-$t$ partition $(S_r, V \setminus S_r)$ bounds $F^*(P, b_r) \le C(S_r)$. If $\max_r C(S_r) < m_{\min}$, candidate $P$ is certifiably excluded using peak masses and discrete incidence alone (0 bytes of intensity retention). $\square$

## Theorem P02 (Exact Rational Farkas Separation Polytopes and Orthogonal Channel Mitosis)

**Statement (`VERIFIED`).** Let two compartment parameter worlds $W_1, W_2$ produce exact rational vector predictions $q^{(1)}, q^{(2)} \in \mathbb{Q}^m$ across $m$ design points under shared channel offset $|b_k| \le B_0$ ($B = 2 B_0$) and residual error $|e_{k,i}| \le \epsilon$. Let $\delta = q^{(1)} - q^{(2)}$, $d_{\min} = \min_i \delta_i$, and $d_{\max} = \max_i \delta_i$.
1. The exact noise supremum $\epsilon^*(W_1, W_2)$ below which $W_1$ and $W_2$ have disjoint observation polytopes is:
   $$\epsilon^*(W_1, W_2) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{d_{\max} - d_{\min}}{4}, & \text{if } |d_{\min} + d_{\max}| \le 2B, \\[8pt] \dfrac{\max(0, |\delta_{\text{ext}}| - B)}{2}, & \text{if } |d_{\min} + d_{\max}| > 2B, \end{cases}$$
   certified by an explicit rational Farkas dual vector $\lambda \in \mathbb{Q}^m$ with $\|\lambda\|_1 \le 1$ satisfying $\sum_i \lambda_i \delta_i - B |\sum_i \lambda_i| = \epsilon^*$.
2. On the PR #78 worlds $S(16,0), C_{25}(11,1/4), C_{50}(6,1/2), C_{75}(1,3/4)$ with $p_L=1/10, p_R=1/5, p_H=2/5$ and $B_0=1/20$, scalar design `LH` achieves $\epsilon^*(\text{LH}; S, C_{50}) = 13/700 > 1/60$ but $\epsilon^*(\text{LH}; S, C_{25}) = 3/448 < 1/60$. Adding the disjoint turnover channel $z(p; r, c) = (rp + 1 - p)/4 - c/8$ on `LH` raises $\min_{W_1 \neq W_2} \epsilon^*_{\text{joint}}$ across all 6 world pairs to $69/320 = 0.215625 > 1/60$.
3. Under two-clone biomarker shedding $m = k_S \Delta V_S + k_R \Delta V_R$ with $k_S = 118/100$ and $k_R = 42/1000$, any clonal step satisfying $\Delta V_S = -1$ and $0 < \Delta V_R < 590/21$ yields $\Delta m < 0$ while $\Delta(V_S + V_R) > 0$, proving $\epsilon^*_{\text{single-marker}} = 0$.

**Proof.**
1. $W_1$ and $W_2$ overlap at noise $\epsilon$ iff $\exists \Delta b \in [-B, B]$ such that $\|\delta + \Delta b \mathbf{1}\|_\infty \le 2\epsilon$. The convex piecewise-linear function $g(\Delta b) = \max_i |\delta_i + \Delta b| = \max(d_{\max} + \Delta b, -d_{\min} - \Delta b)$ attains its unconstrained minimum $(d_{\max} - d_{\min})/2$ at $\Delta b^* = -(d_{\min} + d_{\max})/2$. Clamping $\Delta b^*$ to $[-B, B]$ yields both branches, and setting $\lambda = \frac{1}{4}(e_{i_{\max}} - e_{i_{\min}})$ when $|\Delta b^*| \le B$ (or $\pm \frac{1}{2} e_{i_{\text{ext}}}$ when clamped) achieves exact strong duality $\sum_i \lambda_i \delta_i - B|\sum_i \lambda_i| = \epsilon^*$.
2. Direct rational substitution gives $\delta_L(S, C_{25}) = 16/25 - 53/80 = -9/400$ and $\delta_H(S, C_{25}) = 32/35 - 91/100 = 3/700$, so $(3/700 + 9/400)/4 = 75/11200 = 3/448 < 1/60$. On channel $z$, direct rational substitution gives $\delta_z(S, C_{25}) = (5/32, 9/32, 17/32)$ at $p_L, p_R, p_H$, since $z(1/10; S) - z(1/10; C_{25}) = 5/8 - 15/32 = 5/32$, $z(1/5; S) - z(1/5; C_{25}) = 1 - 23/32 = 9/32$, and $z(2/5; S) - z(2/5; C_{25}) = 7/4 - 39/32 = 17/32$. Then $|d_{\min} + d_{\max}| = |5/32 + 17/32| = 11/16 > 2B = 1/5$, so the clamped branch applies and $\epsilon^*_z(S, C_{25}) = (17/32 - 1/10)/2 = 69/320 > 1/60$, consistent with `certificate.json` (`eps_z_channel_q = 69/320`). Across all 6 pairs the minimum joint separation is $69/320 > 1/60$.
3. Since $k_S / k_R = (118/100) / (42/1000) = 1180/42 = 590/21 > 1$, setting $\Delta V_S = -1$ and $\Delta V_R = 28 \in (1, 590/21)$ gives $\Delta(V_S + V_R) = +27 > 0$ while $\Delta m = -118/100 + 28 \times 42/1000 = -4/1000 < 0$. $\square$

## Theorem P03 (Monotone Pareto-Prefix Pruning Preserves Exact C1/C2 Safe Optimality)

**Statement (`VERIFIED`).** In the discrete rational multi-compartment dynamics $F(s, a)$ where next-state burdens $(v'_S, v'_R)$ and step cost $c(s, a)$ are coordinatewise non-decreasing in $(v_S, v_R)$ and non-increasing in $(h_{\text{core}}, i_{\text{mhc}})$, while next-state reserves $(h'_{\text{core}}, i'_{\text{mhc}})$ are non-decreasing in $(h_{\text{core}}, i_{\text{mhc}})$: if prefix state $s_1$ at depth $t$ satisfies $v_{S,1} \le v_{S,2}$, $v_{R,1} \le v_{R,2}$, $h_1 \ge h_2$, $i_1 \ge i_2$, and accumulated cost $J_1 \le J_2$, then for every suffix sequence $\pi = (a_{t+1}, \dots, a_T)$ that is C1/C2-safe from $s_2$ ($h(\tau) \ge h_{\min}$ and $v_R(\tau) < v_{\text{crit}}$ for all $\tau > t$), $\pi$ is also C1/C2-safe from $s_1$ and achieves total objective $J(s_1, \pi) \le J(s_2, \pi)$.

**Proof.** By induction on suffix step $\tau = t, \dots, T$. Base case $\tau = t$ holds by hypothesis. Assuming the coordinatewise inequality holds at $\tau$, applying the same action $a_{\tau+1}$ preserves $v'_{S,1} \le v'_{S,2}$, $v'_{R,1} \le v'_{R,2} < v_{\text{crit}}$, $h'_1 \ge h'_2 \ge h_{\min}$, $i'_1 \ge i'_2$ (noting that in `SALVAGE_SWITCH`, $i_1 \ge i_2$ implies contraction factor $\gamma(i_1) \le \gamma(i_2)$, reinforcing $v'_{R,1} \le v'_{R,2}$), and $J'_1 \le J'_2$. Thus pruning $s_2$ cannot eliminate all optimal safe trajectories. $\square$
