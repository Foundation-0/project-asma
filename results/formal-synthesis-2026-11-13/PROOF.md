## Theorem `P01`: Canonical Suffix Non-Existence at `PEPMASS = 614.9990` (`z = 3+`) for Peak `#3` (`1514.9196`)

**Statement (`P01`).** Let $\mathcal{M} \subset \mathbb{Q}_{>0}$ be the set of 19 distinct monoisotopic neutral residue masses of the 20 canonical unmodified proteinogenic amino acids (`I` and `L` sharing exact elemental composition $\text{C}_6\text{H}_{11}\text{NO}$, mass $113.08406397713\text{ Da}$), with proton mass $m_p = 1.00727646688\text{ Da}$ and water mass $m_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} = 18.01056468403\text{ Da}$. No nonnegative integer linear combination $\sum_{a \in \mathcal{M}} n_a m_a$ ($n_a \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$) lies in the closed interval $[309.9772,\, 310.1273]\text{ Da}$. Consequently, peak `#3` ($m/z = 1514.9196 \pm 0.015\text{ Da}$, intensity $134{,}437.2$) in [cdr2l-spectrum-locator-2026-11-06.json](../../registries/cdr2l-spectrum-locator-2026-11-06.json) (`PXD023044`, `index=22949`, `SCANS=23848`) cannot be a singly charged canonical `b1+` prefix ion of any unmodified canonical peptide whose triply protonated precursor $m/z$ lies within $\pm 0.020\text{ Th}$ ($\pm 32.5\text{ ppm}$) of `PEPMASS = 614.9990`.

**Proof.** For any unmodified canonical peptide of neutral mass $M = \sum_{i=1}^L m_{a_i} + m_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}$ with triply charged precursor $m/z_3 = (M + 3 m_p)/3 \in [614.9790,\, 615.0190]$, its singly protonated intact mass $[M + \text{H}]^+ = 3\,m/z_3 - 2 m_p$ satisfies
$$[M + \text{H}]^+ \in [1842.922447,\, 1843.042447]\text{ Da}.$$
If an internal cleavage at bond $k \in \{1,\dots,L-1\}$ yields a singly charged prefix acylium ion $b_k^+ = \sum_{i=1}^k m_{a_i} + m_p \in [1514.9046,\, 1514.9346]\text{ Da}$, then subtracting $b_k^+ + m_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}$ from $[M + \text{H}]^+$ forces the nonempty C-terminal suffix residue-mass sum $S_{\text{suf}} = \sum_{i=k+1}^L m_{a_i} = [M + \text{H}]^+ - b_k^+ - m_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}$ into
$$S_{\text{suf}} \in [1842.922447 - 1514.9346 - 18.0105647,\; 1843.042447 - 1514.9046 - 18.0105647] \subset [309.9772,\, 310.1273]\text{ Da}.$$
Since $\min \mathcal{M} = m_{\text{G}} = 57.02146372057\text{ Da}$, any nonnegative integer vector $n \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^{19}$ with $\sum_a n_a m_a \le 310.1273$ satisfies $\sum_a n_a \le \lfloor 310.1273 / 57.02146372057 \rfloor = 5$, restricting the search space to a finite bounded simplex of `2,516` monotone lattice prefix nodes. Exhaustive exact-rational enumeration over all `2,516` nodes (re-checked independently by [analyze_formal_synthesis.py](../../tools/analyze_formal_synthesis.py) and [verify.py](verify.py)) yields zero vectors in $[309.9772,\, 310.1273]\text{ Da}$ (nearest canonical sums are `F+Y = 310.13174 Da` above the upper bound and `H+F+G` / `E+K+S` far outside). $\square$

**Empirical corollary on Scan `23848` (`VERIFIED`).** Pairing peak `#3` (`1514.9196`) with peak `#17` (`329.15015`, matching $y_2^+(\text{YF}) = 329.14958$ at `+1.7 ppm`, alongside $y_1^+(\text{F}) = 166.08662$ `#12` at `+2.2 ppm`) gives $[M + \text{H}]^+ = 1514.9196 + 329.15015 - m_p = 1843.06247\text{ Da}$, i.e. $m/z_3 = 615.02568$ (`+0.02668 Th` from `614.9990`), sitting inside the same quadrupole isolation window and extending backward across `1514.9196 <-[I/L]-- 1401.8372 (#1) <-[N]-- 1287.7947 (#11, -CO -> 1259.7988 #5) <-[V]-- 1188.7234 (#19) <-[P]-- 1091.6687 (#6)` (`35.96%` of total ion current across 19 peaks, completely disjoint from `SQPEYKALFKEIFSR`'s 12-peak `16.80%` primary `b/y` ladder).

## Theorem `P02`: Exact Permutation Unbiasedness and Cross-Fold Calibration Feasibility on Conjecture `C20`

**Statement (`P02`).** Let $P = \{s^+_1,\dots,s^+_M\}$ and $N = \{s^-_1,\dots,s^-_N\}$ be any fixed score multisets ($M, N \ge 1$) with pooled Mann-Whitney AUROC
$$A_{\text{pool}} = \frac{1}{MN} \sum_{i=1}^M \sum_{j=1}^N w(s^+_i, s^-_j), \qquad w(p, q) = \mathbf{1}[p > q] + \tfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{1}[p = q].$$
For any integer $K \ge 1$ and any positive fold-size compositions $m_1 + \dots + m_K = M$ ($m_k \ge 1$) and $n_1 + \dots + n_K = N$ ($n_k \ge 1$), let $\pi = ((P_1, N_1), \dots, (P_K, N_K))$ be drawn uniformly over all $\frac{M!}{\prod_k m_k!}\frac{N!}{\prod_k n_k!}$ stratified partitions with $|P_k| = m_k, |N_k| = n_k$. Then:
1. **Unbiasedness:** $\mathbb{E}_\pi\!\left[\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \text{AUC}(P_k, N_k)\right] = A_{\text{pool}}$ identically in $\mathbb{Q}$, even when fold sizes $(m_k, n_k)$ are unequal across folds.
2. **Cross-fold calibration feasibility (`M = 52, N = 83, A_pool = 3491/4316`):** There exist strictly tie-free score vectors ($135$ distinct rationals in $(0, 1)$, matching [pilot-001-auc-discrepancy.json](../../registries/pilot-001-auc-discrepancy.json)) on balanced 5-fold sizes $m = (11,11,10,10,10)$, $n = (17,17,17,16,16)$ with exact pooled concordance $\frac{3491}{4316} \approx 0.8088507878$ and exact 5-fold mean AUROC $\frac{124513}{149600} \approx 0.832306$ (rounding to `0.83`).

**Proof.** Fix any pair $(i, j) \in \{1,\dots,M\} \times \{1,\dots,N\}$ and fold index $k \in \{1,\dots,K\}$. Because $P_k \subset P$ is a uniformly random subset of size $m_k$ independent of the uniformly random subset $N_k \subset N$ of size $n_k$, $\Pr_\pi(i \in P_k \text{ and } j \in N_k) = \frac{m_k}{M}\cdot\frac{n_k}{N}$. By linearity of expectation,
$$\mathbb{E}_\pi\!\left[\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{1}{m_k n_k} \sum_{i \in P_k}\sum_{j \in N_k} w(s^+_i, s^-_j)\right] = \sum_{i=1}^M \sum_{j=1}^N w(s^+_i, s^-_j) \sum_{k=1}^K \frac{1}{K m_k n_k}\cdot\frac{m_k}{M}\cdot\frac{n_k}{N} = \frac{1}{MN}\sum_{i=1}^M\sum_{j=1}^N w(s^+_i, s^-_j) = A_{\text{pool}},$$
since $m_k n_k$ cancels identically in every summand $k$. Part (2) is established constructively by the explicit 135-rational witness in [report.json](report.json) (verified independently by [verify.py](verify.py)): adding fold-constant calibration offsets $\delta_k$ leaves every within-fold order $s^+_i - s^-_j = (u^+_i + \delta_k) - (u^-_j + \delta_k) = u^+_i - u^-_j$ invariant while shifting cross-fold pairs $(k \ne \ell)$, showing the two clauses of Conjecture `C20` are compatible on a single unmodified out-of-fold score vector. $\square$

## Theorem `P03`: Universal Composition Non-Identifiability and Exact Sequence-Reversal Law for Two-Compartment Cytoreduction/Chemotherapy Models

**Statement (`P03`).** For initial sensitive and resistant burdens $x_0 = (s_0, r_0) \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}^2$ with total $T_0 = s_0 + r_0$, diagonal chemotherapy operator $C(s, r) = (a s, b r)$ with $a, b \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}$, proportional surgery $\Pi_q(s, r) = (q s, q r)$ for $q \in (0, 1] \cap \mathbb{Q}$, and capped-residual surgery $\Sigma_\tau(s, r) = \min\!\left(1, \frac{\tau}{s+r}\right)(s, r)$ for $\tau \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}$ (extending `tools/analyze_treatment_sequence.py`):
1. **Proportional commutativity:** $C(\Pi_q(x_0)) = (q a s_0, q b r_0) = \Pi_q(C(x_0))$.
2. **Universal composition non-identifiability:** For all $\tau_1, \tau_2 \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}$, all four terminal states $\Pi_q(C(x_0))$, $C(\Pi_q(x_0))$, $C(\Sigma_{\tau_1}(x_0))$, and $\Sigma_{\tau_2}(C(x_0))$ are nonzero and have identical resistant fraction
$$\rho_{\text{post}} = \frac{R_{\text{final}}}{S_{\text{final}} + R_{\text{final}}} = \frac{b r_0}{a s_0 + b r_0}.$$
3. **Matched-cap ratio and unequal-cap reversal boundary:** Let $g = \frac{a s_0 + b r_0}{T_0} > 0$. For $\text{before} = C(\Sigma_{\tau_1}(x_0))$ and $\text{after} = \Sigma_{\tau_2}(C(x_0))$:
   - If $\tau_1 = \tau_2 = q T_0$ with $q \in (0, 1]$, then $\frac{\text{after}_R}{\text{before}_R} = \min\!\left(\frac{1}{q}, \frac{1}{g}\right)$.
   - For arbitrary $\tau_1, \tau_2 \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}$, $\text{after}_R < \text{before}_R \iff \tau_2 < g \cdot \min(T_0, \tau_1)$.

**Proof.** (1) Multiplication in $\mathbb{Q}$ is commutative and associative: $a(q s_0) = q(a s_0)$ and $b(q r_0) = q(b r_0)$.
(2) Let $\lambda_1 = \min(1, \tau_1 / T_0) > 0$ and $\lambda_2 = \min(1, \tau_2 / (g T_0)) > 0$. Since $C$ is linear over $\mathbb{Q}$, $C(\Sigma_{\tau_1}(x_0)) = C(\lambda_1 x_0) = \lambda_1 (a s_0, b r_0)$, whereas $\Sigma_{\tau_2}(C(x_0)) = \lambda_2 (a s_0, b r_0)$, and $\Pi_q(C(x_0)) = C(\Pi_q(x_0)) = q(a s_0, b r_0)$. Because every terminal state is a positive scalar multiple $\mu (a s_0, b r_0)$ ($\mu > 0$) of the same vector $(a s_0, b r_0)$ with $a s_0 + b r_0 > 0$, $\mu$ cancels identically in $\frac{\mu b r_0}{\mu a s_0 + \mu b r_0} = \frac{b r_0}{a s_0 + b r_0}$. Thus relative post-treatment composition alone cannot distinguish proportional from capped surgery or upfront from interval sequencing without an absolute-scale burden measurement.
(3) Since $b r_0 > 0$, $\frac{\text{after}_R}{\text{before}_R} = \frac{\lambda_2 b r_0}{\lambda_1 b r_0} = \frac{\min(1, \tau_2 / (g T_0))}{\min(1, \tau_1 / T_0)}$. Setting $\tau_1 = \tau_2 = q T_0$ ($0 < q \le 1$) yields $\lambda_1 = q$ and $\lambda_2 = \min(1, q/g)$, so $\lambda_2 / \lambda_1 = \min(1/q, 1/g)$. For general $\tau_1, \tau_2 > 0$, $\lambda_1 \le 1$ implies $\min(1, \tau_2 / (g T_0)) < \lambda_1$ cannot hold if $\tau_2 / (g T_0) \ge 1$ (since $1 \not< \lambda_1$); hence $\lambda_2 < \lambda_1 \iff \frac{\tau_2}{g T_0} < \min\!\left(1, \frac{\tau_1}{T_0}\right) \iff \tau_2 < g \cdot \min(T_0, \tau_1)$. $\square$
